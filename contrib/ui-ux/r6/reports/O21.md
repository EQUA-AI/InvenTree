Routine findings do not appear as a previous closed recurrence.
- Native title/observation/optional reading/step form in the existing fault disclosure. Reuses the actor/host-scoped, persisted, cross-tab-serialized process command controller: pending, confirmed, refused, uncertain and exact replay. Reload never auto-submits. Recovery permission refusal, transport failure and mismatched receipt retain uncertainty. Exact stale-version replay proves noncommit, permits latest-record review and deliberate fresh intent. Refused drafts survive reload/review. Viewer links are read-only; both History panels show the saved relation.
