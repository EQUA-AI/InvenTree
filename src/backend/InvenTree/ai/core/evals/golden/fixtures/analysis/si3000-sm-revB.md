## 3. Preventive maintenance

- Inspect the DC compartment every 6 months (interval halved from Rev A).
- Frame bolt torque: 38 Nm, star pattern (raised from Rev A).
- Coolant loop flush interval: 36 months.

## 4. Alarms

DC overvoltage alarms require a string-level insulation survey before any
reset. Refer isolation and de-energization to the site safety authority.
The alarm reset sequence is a controlled procedure: see the safety
authority's approved work instruction; it is not reproduced here.
