## Oil system

The ZR-9 oil sump capacity is **12.5 litres** of PAO-46 synthetic compressor
oil. Change the oil every 4,000 running hours or 12 months, whichever comes
first.

## Discharge limits

Trip the unit at a discharge temperature of 110 °C. The safety valve is set
at 14 bar.
